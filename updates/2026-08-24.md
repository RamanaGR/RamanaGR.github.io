### VIALS: A Benchmark for Visual Interpretation of Artifacts in the Life Sciences
**Source:** arXiv  
**Insight:** This paper introduces a new accelerated Newton method for minimizing convex functions with Lipschitz continuous Hessian, which achieves a global convergence rate of \(O(1/k^3)\) using only one linear solve per iteration. The method is innovative as it is the first second-order approach to attain this rate without solving auxiliary nonlinear subproblems or using dual corrections.
