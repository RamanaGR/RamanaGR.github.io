### VLK: Learning Humanoid Loco-Manipulation from Synthetic Interactions in Reconstructed Scenes
**Source:** arXiv  
**Insight:** The authors propose a method for generating vision-language-kinematics (VLK) supervision synthetically in reconstructed scenes, allowing for the creation of large-scale datasets of synchronized egocentric images, language commands, and robot-compatible kinematic trajectories. This approach leverages 3D Gaussian Splatting to reconstruct indoor environments and synthesize navigation and object-interaction trajectories using privileged scene information.
