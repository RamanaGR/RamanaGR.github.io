### AgentSSL: Can MLE Agents Leverage Unlabeled Data?
**Source:** arXiv (https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17323v1)
**Insight:** AgentSSL demonstrates that machine learning agents can effectively leverage unlabeled data to synthesize and adapt semi-supervised learning techniques, outperforming human-engineered methods in many scenarios. However, program selection remains a critical bottleneck, with performance drops when relying on the agent's feedback metrics.
