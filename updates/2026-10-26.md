### SceneBloom: Agentic Indoor Environment Generation with Realistic and Diverse Layouts for Embodied Learning
**Source:** arXiv
**Insight:** SceneBloom is a framework that combines visual layout sampling with contract-guided 3D compilation to generate realistic and diverse indoor environments. This approach achieves high visual quality and physical stability, with no mesh collisions or out-of-bounds objects, making it effective for training embodied agents in simulated settings.
