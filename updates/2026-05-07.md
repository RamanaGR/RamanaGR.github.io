### Taming Outlier Tokens in Diffusion Transformers
**Source:** arXiv
**Insight:** This paper investigates and addresses the issue of outlier tokens in Diffusion Transformers (DiTs) used for image generation. The authors demonstrate that both pretrained Vision Transformer encoders and DiT models themselves can generate high-norm tokens with limited local information, which negatively impacts generation quality. They introduce Dual-Stage Registers (DSR) as a register-based intervention to mitigate these outlier artifacts, leading to improved performance across various datasets.
