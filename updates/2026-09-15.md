### E2A-Bench: Benchmarking Evidence-to-Action Reliability in Financial Chart Reasoning
**Source:** arXiv
**Insight:** E2A-Bench is a benchmark for evaluating financial vision-language models (VLMs) by assessing their ability to turn chart evidence into reliable action recommendations. The benchmark evaluates grounding, reasoning-action consistency, evidence-confidence calibration, and directional coverage, highlighting that existing evaluations focusing solely on hallucination scores may overlook critical issues such as low directional coverage and imbalanced BUY:SELL ratios.
