### HANDOFF: Humanoid Agentic Task-Space Whole-Body Control via Distilled Complementary Teachers
**Source:** arXiv
**Insight:** The paper presents a novel controller called HANDOFF for humanoid robots, which uses a compact command space interface that is easier for task planners to generate. This interface is learned through multi-teacher knowledge distillation from three specialists: whole-body motion tracking with safety filters, locomotion, and fall recovery. HANDOFF achieves state-of-the-art performance in velocity tracking and offers a large manipulation workspace on the Unitree G1 robot.
