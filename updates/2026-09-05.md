### Compile by Training: Turning Natural-Language Specifications into Local Neural Functions
**Source:** arXiv  
**Insight:** This research introduces a method called compile by training that converts natural-language specifications into reusable neural functions. By generating task-specific examples at compile time, it trains a small adapter for a compact interpreter, allowing the function to run locally without dependency on remote models. While this approach offers higher semantic accuracy compared to existing methods, it comes with a longer compile-time cost of about one minute.
