### Selectivity and Shape in the Design of Forward-Forward Goodness Functions
**Source:** arXiv
**Insight:** The study explores the impact of selectivity and activity shape on the Forward-Forward (FF) algorithm, introducing burstiness goodness, a parameter-free, scale-invariant excess-kurtosis objective that enhances FF learning by rewarding heavy-tailed activity patterns. This leads to significant accuracy improvements across small-image datasets like Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-10.
