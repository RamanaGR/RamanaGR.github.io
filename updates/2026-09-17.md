### Objective vs. Search: Decomposing What Makes a Good Tokeniser
**Source:** arXiv
**Insight:** The study introduces two new tokenisation algorithms, BottomUpLL and TopDownComp, to disentangle the effects of optimisation objective (compression vs. log-likelihood) and search procedure (bottom-up merging vs. top-down pruning) on language model performance. The results indicate that the search procedure is the dominant factor in achieving lower bits-per-byte, while the tokeniser design choices show no consistent relationship with performance on the BLiMP task.
