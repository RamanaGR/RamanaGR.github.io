### Faster Results from a Smarter Schedule: Reframing Collegiate Cross Country through Analysis of the National Running Club Database
**Source:** arXiv
**Insight:** The study reveals that individual improvement in collegiate cross country cannot be reliably predicted using race results, suggesting that current scheduling practices rely heavily on intuition rather than data-driven insights. However, team race frequency is positively correlated with nationals placement, highlighting the importance of a strategic schedule in achieving better performance.
