### On Agentic Manipulation Policies
**Source:** arXiv
**Insight:** Generalist AI models, such as agentic manipulation policies (AMPs), can control manipulators more reliably than specialist policies, achieving state-of-the-art results on benchmarks without task-specific training. AMPs adapt their actions in real-time, developing strategies like searching for hidden objects and using tools, and can learn from experience with added memory, significantly reducing failure rates in real-world tasks.
