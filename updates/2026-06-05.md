### HANDOFF: Humanoid Agentic Task-Space Whole-Body Control via Distilled Complementary Teachers
**Source:** arXiv
**Insight:** This paper introduces HANDOFF, a novel whole-body controller for humanoid robots that uses a compact interface for task-space manipulation. By distilling knowledge from three complementary specialists—whole-body motion tracking, locomotion, and fall-recovery—HANDOFF achieves state-of-the-art velocity tracking and provides a large robust manipulation workspace on the Unitree G1 robot.
