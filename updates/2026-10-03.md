### Replacing Large Language Models with Jev Decision Models for Low-Latency Edge Service Orchestration
**Source:** arXiv
**Insight:** The integration of Jev's decision-oriented API into edge service orchestration reduces decision latency by 22.7-64.5% compared to large language models, with consistent performance across various input sizes and contract widths. Jev is more cost-effective than LLMs for bounded contracts, maintaining high accuracy and on-time request rates even at high loads.
