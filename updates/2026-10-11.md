### On the estimation and validity of AI time horizons---a statistical look at the METR plot
**Source:** arXiv
**Insight:** The study redefines AI time horizons using splines and item-response theory, revealing that the relationship between human completion time and AI difficulty is non-linear. The resulting spline model shows that a time-horizon jump from 3 to 30 minutes is easier than one from 30 minutes to 5 hours, despite the same multiplier, indicating the importance of considering the non-linear relationship when interpreting AI capabilities.
